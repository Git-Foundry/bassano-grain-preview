Packaging & shipping

Plan your shipment

How can my order be packed?

Discuss cleaning, sizing, custom packaging or one-tonne totes with the sales team. Include your processing or resale requirements.

Can I request bulk loading?

Ask about 20-foot and 40-foot containers or bulk vessel loading. Include the destination and delivery window so the team can assess availability.

What should an international buyer provide?

Share the destination country and port, quantity, grade and required documents. The team can confirm the available options for your shipment.
